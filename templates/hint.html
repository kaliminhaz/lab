Three Nudges, Ranked

Try to solve it with just Hint 1. Only open the next one if you're truly stuck.

Hint 1 — Where does your text actually land?

Go back and look at the terminal block that appeared under the form after your last attempt. Your username got pasted between two single quotes. What happens if your input contains a single quote?

Hint 2 — Ending the string early

A lone ' in the username field closes the quoted string ahead of schedule. Everything you type after that isn't treated as data anymore — it becomes part of the actual SQL logic the database executes.

Hint 3 — Making the question unanswerable-as-false

Databases love OR. If you close the username string and then add OR 1=1, you've created a condition that's always true, no matter what the real username is. Add -- right after to comment out the rest of the original query (including the password check). Put it together as the username: a single quote, then OR 1=1 --, with a space before and after.
